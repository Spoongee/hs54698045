Drop music in this folder and Absolute AFK Cinema will play it while the camera runs.

.ogg  .mp3  .wav  .flac  .opus  .weba  .aiff  .au - no converting needed. The mod
decodes the file itself and hands the samples to Minecraft, so your track goes
through the normal Music slider, pauses with the game and stops when you come
back, exactly like anything else the game plays.

You can drop a file in while the game is running - the folder is watched, so the
new track joins in by itself and plays as the next one. No restart, no button.

PLAYLISTS
A subfolder is a playlist: music/night, music/building, whatever you call it.
An .m3u file is a playlist too, and the better one, because its lines may point
anywhere on this computer:

    #EXTINF:0,Evening at the lake
    D:/Music/Ambient/evening at the lake.flac
    ../another folder/second track.mp3

That way the music collection you already have is played where it already lies,
and nothing has to be copied into a game folder. Lines starting with # are notes;
an #EXTINF line gives the track the name you write after the comma.

In the settings screen (F9 -> Music) you say which playlists to draw from - leave
that empty for all of them - whether you hear your own tracks, the three that come
with the mod, or both, and whether the game's music discs join in.

File names are yours; nothing here renames anything.
